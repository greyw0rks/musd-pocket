# Pocket — Wave 2 submission draft

**Track 2: Access and Distribution for MUSD/MEZO.**

Pocket lets users send/receive mUSD, pay merchant invoices, borrow against BTC and manage Mezo Savings, swaps, locks and liquidity through social sign-in and an embedded wallet.

## Contribution

Merchant profiles, links and QR checkout give mUSD a payment destination. The payer signs directly; Pocket verifies exact transfer proof and records invoice and Activity together. Pending payments recover from their saved hash without another transfer; legacy vault settlement remains isolated.

Savings includes uncheckpointed global-index yield, fixing the false-zero claim display. Swap/Wealth adds verified-pool swaps, liquidity management/fee claims and MEZO lock lifecycle. The approved 20-testnet-MEZO welcome campaign is deployed and funded with 100 MEZO for five claims; its first verified pilot claim is pending.

## Revenue

Proposed disclosed merchant fees partly funding BTC gas sponsorship and operations, with premium reporting after pilot demand. Ordinary P2P sends are not the initial fee target. Pricing, sponsorship and fee collection are not implemented; no revenue is claimed.
