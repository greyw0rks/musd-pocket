pocket.01 / THE PROBLEM

Bitcoin capital needs everyday uses.

Mezo lets Bitcoin holders borrow mUSD. Everyday payments still need a clear recipient, a familiar checkout and a reason to acquire that money.

Access

Social sign-in and an embedded wallet make the first payment easier.

Use

A merchant invoice gives mUSD a destination beyond transfers among existing holders.

Confidence

Exact amounts, verified receipts and clear recovery help users trust the outcome.

Pocket turns Mezo access into a practical payment flow.01 / 10
pocket.02 / THE PRODUCT

Pocket. Send. Request. Get paid.

One mobile app for mUSD payments and Mezo money management. Users keep funds in their own Privy embedded wallets.

Home

Send to a username or wallet, create an invoice and follow Activity.

Swap

Exchange mUSD with BTC, mUSDC or mUSDT through verified Mezo testnet pools.

Wealth

Manage Savings, MEZO locks and liquidity pools. Borrow against BTC from Home.

pocket.03 / MERCHANT CHECKOUT

A payment link that settles in mUSD.

A business creates an invoice, shares its link or QR and receives mUSD directly. Checkout keeps the invoice open through sign-in.

Create

Business profile, exact mUSD price, description and expiry. Each invoice preserves its merchant-name snapshot.

Pay

The customer checks the recipient and amount, then signs one direct wallet transfer.

Confirm

Pocket verifies the transaction and records the paid invoice and Activity together. Recovery uses the existing hash.

Checkout is built. Independent merchant/customer demand still needs pilot evidence.03 / 10
pocket.04 / ARCHITECTURE

Wallet to wallet, verified on Mezo.

The user wallet holds and signs the funds. Postgres records profiles, invoices and payment outcomes; it does not create spendable money.

User wallet

Privy sign-in resolves the embedded wallet. Direct payments do not use PocketVault.

Mezo

mUSD moves on-chain. The payer uses BTC for gas; the recipient needs no transaction.

Pocket API

Verify sender, token, exact recipient/amount, calldata, receipt and Transfer log. Prevent invoice-proof reuse.

Legacy vault reconciliation is isolated from direct transfers.04 / 10

pocket.06 / SWAP AND WEALTH

Manage Mezo assets inside Pocket.

Reviews show amounts, fees, slippage, minimum output and deadlines. Each approval and final action gets a separate user confirmation.

Swaps and pools

mUSD/BTC, mUSD/mUSDC and mUSD/mUSDT. Add/remove liquidity and claim pool fees; empty pairs cannot swap.

MEZO locks

Create, top up, extend or withdraw eligible locks. Permanent Start unlock starts a new 208-week wait.

Recovery

Save pending actions by wallet and chain. Check the existing receipt after reload without signing again.

A live liquidity addition is confirmed. Fork and wallet fixtures cover broader lifecycle behavior.06 / 10
